# 360 View — Worked Example: "Why do I feel low energy all the time?"

Built from live web research, August 2026. This is the health-vertical seed example for the 360 feature: one durable QUESTION, ~15 competing ANSWERS, a monetization layer riding the current winner, upstream meta-narratives, verified mutation lineages, and the (sparse) shared clocks.

---

## 1. The Question (the durable object)

**Canonical form:** "Why do I feel low energy all the time?"

**Fingerprints (exact phrases people type):** "why am I always tired" · "tired all the time" · "wake up exhausted after 8 hours" · "no energy no motivation" · "always tired even after sleeping" · "cortisol face" · "ferritin face" · "how to fix your mitochondria" · "adrenal fatigue symptoms" · "why am I so tired all of a sudden"

**Demand profile:** Effectively permanent. Fatigue is among the most common primary-care complaints and is medically underexplained often enough that there is a perpetual vacuum for mechanism narratives. The question predates every answer on this map and will outlive all of them — which is exactly why it is the right unit of tracking, not any single answer.

**Current king of the hill (mid-2026):** Mitochondrial dysfunction. It has the celebrity book (Casey Means' *Good Energy*), a pending cabinet-adjacent nomination, a Harvard psychiatry wing (Chris Palmer's *Brain Energy*), and the richest monetization stack (Section 3).

## 4. FEEDS — upstream meta-narratives

1. **"Modern life is poisoning you."** The master frame. Feeds mitochondria, seed oils, microplastics, mold, cortisol, and light-deficiency simultaneously — each is a different *mechanism of the same poisoning*. In 2025–26 this frame achieved something rare: **state sponsorship** via the MAHA Commission ("childhood chronic disease epidemic"; Sept 2025 strategy with 120+ initiatives; 42 states with MAHA-aligned legislation; Jan 2026 Dietary Guidelines shifting toward traditional fats).
2. **"Mainstream medicine treats symptoms, not root causes."** The functional-medicine frame. It is the delivery vehicle for adrenal fatigue → HPA axis, mold, parasites, and the mitochondria answer itself. Every answer on this map that lacks institutional recognition survives by routing through this frame.
3. **"Your body is data — measure it yourself."** The quantified-self frame. Feeds CGMs, wearables, ferritin self-testing, hormone panels, sleep scores. Distinct because it feeds *falsifiable-at-n-of-1* answers, which behave differently on a receipt strip.

## 5. MUTATES_INTO — verified lineage

**Hypothesis tested: "adrenal fatigue (2000s) → mitochondria (2020s)." Verdict: partially confirmed — the lineage is real but it forked, and the mitochondria answer is better modeled as a competing successor than a rebrand.**

What actually happened to "adrenal fatigue" (term coined by chiropractor James Wilson, 1998):

- **Fork A — clinical rebrand:** systematic reviews found no evidence of "exhausted adrenals"; functional medicine retreated to **"HPA axis dysfunction"** (signaling problem, not gland failure). A 2025 *American Journal of Medicine* paper now formalizes HPA-axis dysfunction as a syndrome while explicitly advising clinicians to avoid the term "adrenal fatigue." The mechanism migrated upward (glands → brain-gland signaling) to escape falsification — a textbook mutation.
- **Fork B — mass-market rebrand:** the same demand resurfaced on TikTok as **"cortisol face" / "cortisol detox" / "cortisol cocktails"** (2024→, ~800M views). Endocrinologists refute it on the record (normal stress does not produce Cushingoid features). Same claim shape as adrenal fatigue, new vocabulary, new platform.
- **The mitochondria answer** did not descend from adrenal fatigue; it arrived via a different lineage (functional medicine + longevity science + Palmer/Means) and is now **outcompeting** the cortisol lineage for the same question — though the ecosystem happily co-sells both (retail content literally titles them together: "adrenal fatigue AND mitochondrial dysfunction").

**Second verified lineage:** ME/CFS (1980s, long dismissed) → **long COVID** (2020→) — the same symptom cluster finally acquiring institutional research legitimacy, now itself feeding the mitochondria answer (mitochondrial-dysfunction papers in long COVID are the mito narrative's strongest real science).

**Third (weaker):** candida overgrowth (1980s–90s) → parasite cleanses (2020s TikTok) — same "hidden organism steals your energy" shape, ~30–50% symptom overlap in its own marketing.

## 7. Receipt-strip assessment per answer (falsifiability audit)

| Answer | Datable claims? | Falsifiable? | What the strip would show |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mitochondria | Almost none | Only via rider RCTs | "Mechanism narrative — no falsifiable deadline"; rider layer scores independently (urolithin A: supported-narrow; NAD+: unsupported; methylene blue: unsupported + safety flags) |
| Cortisol detox | None | Core claims refuted on record | Institutional verdict REFUTED ("cortisol face" not endocrinologically possible from normal stress) vs. 800M-view volume — the flagship honesty-gap tile |
| Glucose spikes | Per-person, yes | **Self-verifying at n=1** (CGM) | Novel strip type: individual data real, population benefit for healthy users contested (Johns Hopkins) |
| Sleep debt | Few | Broadly supported | Highest mainstream-consensus answer on the map |
| Low testosterone | Yes — "25% decline 1999–2016" is checkable | Per-person via bloodwork | Decline datapoint real; "TRT for tired young men" contested; commercial-incentive flag (telehealth DTC) |
| Seed oils | Some | Yes, slowly (population lipids) | Best receipt on the map: predictor's own MAHA report **declined to call seed oils harmful** while its champion calls them poison — a self-refutation entry |
| Gut microbiome | Some (ZOE trials) | Partially | Supported-but-oversold; ZOE's own 2026 trial undercut single-strain probiotic sellers |
| Ferritin | Per-person, yes | **Blood test** | Real underdiagnosis (≈38% of reproductive-age women) + contested threshold (15/30/50 ng/mL) — honest strip shows both |
| Vitamin D | Per-person, yes | Yes — and largely failed | Deficiency real; supplementation RCTs mostly null for fatigue; Mendelian randomization finds no causal effect. REFUTED-leaning yet immortal — key exhibit that refutation doesn't kill narratives |
| Microplastics | Yes (NEJM 2024 plaque study, 4.5x) | Evidence evolving; detox products unfalsifiable | Split strip: exposure findings real and datable; "PEMF cellular detox" layer evidence-free |
| Long COVID/ME-CFS | Yes (biomarker literature) | Yes, actively | Most institutionally legitimate; lowest algorithmic reach — inverse of cortisol |
| Light/circadian | Few | Circadian core supported; "quantum" wing unfalsifiable | Two-wing strip: Huberman wing vs. Kruse wing under one answer |
| Mold | No | Not at population level | CDC/AAAAI non-recognition vs. functional-med clinic economy |
| Parasites | Per-person (stool test) | Yes — and generally comes back negative | REFUTED; doctors on record warning the trend is unfounded and risky |
| Dopamine burnout | No | Metaphor, mostly | Unfalsifiable framing; 2026 "digital burnout scale" gives it a first measurable edge |

**Design finding for the feature:** the finance-born receipt strip assumes dated predictions; most health answers make *timeless mechanism claims* instead, so the prediction ledger will sit empty. The health vertical needs three added gates: **(1) institutional verdict** (recognized / contested / refuted by named bodies), **(2) n-of-1 testability** (is there a blood test/CGM/sleep study that settles it *for you* — ferritin yes, mitochondria no), and **(3) rider-layer score** (evidence and price of what's being sold on the answer's back). Born-date, recycled-share, source-spread, and mutation-lineage gates transfer unchanged — and mutation lineage is arguably *more* valuable here than in finance, because "this exact claim shape failed under a previous name" (adrenal fatigue → cortisol detox) is the single most protective fact a tired person searching at 1 a.m. could see.

**Suggested first tiles:** mitochondria (the king), cortisol detox (the honesty-gap flagship), seed oils (best clocks), glucose spikes (novel self-verifying type), low testosterone. **Shadow stubs:** parasites, mold, quantum light, microplastics-detox, ferritin face.

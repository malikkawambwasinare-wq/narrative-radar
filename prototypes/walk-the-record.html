Chapter 04 · The Tests

Reality answered. Here's how.

Two claims met their deadlines and failed — no 1937 repeat (markets went on to record highs), no 2022 "perfect storm" (inflation fell, equities rallied). Three landed in the grey: a recession did arrive in 2020, but through a pandemic, not the predicted debt mechanism — timing credit without mechanism credit. And the engine itself has mutated six times, tracking the news cycle: 1937 → debt supercycle → internal conflict → Phase 5 → capital war → AI capex.

## 5. Reading A — a qualifying event occurred inside the horizon

The Senate took up H.R. 3633 on the floor on 2026-08-07, inside the window. The majority leader made
a motion to proceed on the floor, the clerk read it, and a cloture motion carrying 17 signatures was
filed on the bill. In ordinary usage, a bill that is moved to on the Senate floor and made the subject
of a cloture filing has "reached the floor." The claimant's phrase was "get to a full senate floor
vote"; on this reading, *getting to* a vote describes arrival at floor consideration, which the
Congressional Record shows occurred, with the vote itself fixed by unanimous consent for a date
certain. Armstrong's stated mechanism — the recess as a forcing function that brings people to the
table at the last minute — is consistent with action being taken on the final day before adjournment.

## 6. Reading B — no qualifying event occurred inside the horizon

The claim names a specific parliamentary event: a full Senate floor vote. The Congressional Record
page recording the 2026-08-07 proceedings on H.R. 3633 shows a motion to proceed and a cloture filing,
and no vote of any kind on that motion or the bill. The Daily Digest itemizes nine record votes that
day and none is on H.R. 3633. By unanimous consent the cloture vote was fixed for 2026-09-15, after
the horizon closed and after the Senate's return. Congress.gov's action list for H.R. 3633 records no
Senate vote at any point. On this reading the events of 2026-08-07 are steps toward a vote rather than
the vote named in the claim.
